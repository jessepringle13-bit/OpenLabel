# Evidence review packet: sweeteners and added sugar (2026-10-03)
Status: provisional, owner review pending (Jesse Pringle). Built, not Confirmed.

## Records and recommended colors
| Record | Claim | Recommended color | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| aspartame-cancer | Aspartame at usual intakes causes cancer | Yellow, unresolved | IARC Group 2B (limited liver cancer evidence) vs JECFA and EFSA finding no convincing evidence; cohort association only |
| sweeteners-weight-disease | Non-sugar sweeteners help long-term weight control or lower chronic disease risk | Yellow, contested | Small short-term weight difference; WHO conditional advice against; observational harms at low certainty; industry-linked favorable review |
| added-sugar-dental-caries | Added sugars above about 10 percent of energy raise tooth decay risk | Orange, converging | WHO strong recommendation, moderate evidence; high certainty for sugary drinks |
| sugary-drinks-weight-diabetes | Sugar-sweetened drinks raise weight gain and type 2 diabetes risk | Orange, converging | Trials plus cohorts, moderate certainty, about one can a day |
