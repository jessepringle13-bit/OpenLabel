# Evidence review packet: seed oils (round 1, 2026-10-03)
Owner: Jesse Pringle. Prepared by OpenLabel research. Record: seed-oils-inflammation (evidence-register.js). Dossier: docs/dossiers/seed-oils-inflammation.md. Status: provisional, owner review pending.

## What changed in the record
- 10 sources with links (previously 2, no links). Funding and interests read for 9 of 10.
- Added the strongest human evidence on the actual claim: a 2017 meta-analysis of 30 randomized trials (1,377 people) with no effect on CRP, IL-6 or TNF-alpha, and a 2012 review of 15 trials.
- Added a gene-variant signal (FADS1, 62 men) and a meta-regression hint that CRP might rise at very high intake, both on the "for" side and labelled as signals.
- Added heart-outcome evidence on both sides, labelled as a separate question: Sydney and Minnesota re-analyses, Cochrane (null), Hamley (no benefit in well-controlled trials), AHA 2017 (about 30 percent fewer events), Marklund 2019.
- Funding flags: Johnson 2012 (ILSI lipids committee; Monsanto and Bunge consulting), Marklund 2019 (Unilever restricted grant; Bunge and others), AHA 2017 members (canola, walnut, beef and drug industry ties).

## Checks
Standing: Crossref lists no correction or retraction for Johnson 2012, Su 2017, Marklund 2019, Ramsden 2013 and 2016, Hamley 2017. AHA 2017 has a disclosure-only correction. Cochrane has a newer version with the same main results.

## Still open
