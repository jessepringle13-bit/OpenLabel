## 0. Claims (split because they have different evidence)
- A. Aspartame at usual intakes causes cancer in people. Scope: adults and children, usual dietary intake, cancer incidence.
- B. Using non-sugar sweeteners instead of sugar helps long-term weight control or lowers chronic disease risk. Scope: regular use within acceptable daily intakes; adults and children; weight, type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, mortality.
Not claimed: anything about sugar alcohols (erythritol, xylitol), people with diabetes, or individual sweeteners other than aspartame in A.

## 5. Outcome
- A (aspartame and cancer): status unresolved, yellow. Shows: hazard classification Group 2B; regulators find no convincing evidence; cohort association. Does not show: risk at any amount; cause. Would change: IARC/JECFA reassessment, a second large cohort, or mechanism data.
- B (sweeteners for weight and disease): status contested, yellow. Shows: WHO conditional advice against use for weight control; small trial weight difference; observational links at low certainty. Does not show: cause; effects of any one sweetener; sugar alcohols. Would change: long-term randomized trials with hard outcomes.
Concern by context: the evidence is about regular use; per-product amount is not on the label.
